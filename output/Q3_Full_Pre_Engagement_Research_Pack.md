- Meridian Financial Group plc is a mid-size financial services group headquartered in London. [CL-01, Section 1 — Group Overview]

### Protiviti Point of View: Growth versus Control Capacity

- Firms pursuing growth in regulated activity frequently under-invest in control capacity, assuming that existing controls will scale. Control capacity is the binding constraint, and supervisors increasingly test whether capability was scaled in advance of volume. [PV-08, The Pattern We See]
- Growth is approved on the basis of a remediation programme reported as complete, although closure evidence has not been independently validated. [PV-08, Three Failure Modes]
- Control resourcing plans lag the commercial plan by twelve to eighteen months. [PV-08, Three Failure Modes]
- Governance forums are constituted after deployment rather than before it, most visibly in artificial intelligence adoption. [PV-08, Three Failure Modes]
- Good practice looks like this: independent validation of remediation closure before the programme is stood down; an explicit control capacity plan, linked to the commercial growth plan, with volume triggers; governance established as a precondition of deployment; and board reporting that distinguishes management assertion from independently validated position. [PV-08, What Good Looks Like]

## 2. Conflicts and Reconciliation

**Subject:** Status of the financial crime remediation programme (complete vs. high-priority findings still open)

- **Source A:** Q3 2025 board minutes record the programme as 'substantially complete'. All twelve high-priority findings are remediated and closed with second line acceptance, and the Head of Compliance confirmed that no high-priority findings remain open. Only three medium-priority findings are open. [CL-05, Item 6 — Financial Crime Remediation Programme]
- **Source B:** Press report citing persons familiar with supervisory dialogue. Supervisors do not regard the programme as complete, and four high-priority findings from the 2024 supervisory review remain open (beneficial ownership verification for complex fund structures, sanctions screening tuning). Some findings previously reported closed were reopened after independent validation testing found the closure evidence insufficient. [EX-07, Remediation Status Questioned]
- **Resolution basis:** unresolved
- **Resolution:** Neither source can be preferred on the evidence. CL-05 is the Group's own record, written by the party whose assertion is in question. External sources (EX-02, EX-03) say closure is often evidenced by management assertion, and CL-01 gives a more cautious statement than the minutes. EX-07 relies on anonymous, secondhand sources and has no direct supervisory document. The two may differ in timing, because EX-07 may describe later reopenings after the Q3 2025 minutes. The documents do not show whether that is the case. Corroboration is also thin: CL-08 and PV-09 show the sanctions tuning was last reviewed in 2023, deferred, and carried forward, which is consistent with EX-07 but does not prove that findings were reopened. Independent closure validation (see PV-04) is the way to settle it.

**Subject:** Core platform modernisation programme: 'on track' vs. delayed and over budget

- **Source A:** The investor presentation describes technology modernisation as on track for a 62% cost-to-income ratio by 2028. [CL-10, Key Messages to Market]
- **Source B:** Board minutes record the programme as about four months behind baseline. Data platform migration wave one has slipped to Q2 2026, and forecast spend has risen by GBP 14m. [CL-06, Item 4 — Core Platform Modernisation Programme]
- **Resolution basis:** authority/independence
- **Resolution:** CL-06 is the more reliable source on programme status. It is detailed internal board reporting with specific figures, and it is corroborated by CL-08 (wave one delayed to Q2 2026), EX-08 (programme already reporting slippage) and PV-05 (optimistic milestone reporting). CL-10 is promotional investor messaging with no supporting data. The Group's own investor materials therefore understate known slippage. The 62% target itself is not contradicted.
