### Assurance Methodology and Practitioner Perspective

- Protiviti's risk-based assurance approach treats closure validation as a distinct assurance activity. Closure evidence is tested independently against the original finding. A finding counts as closed only where the underlying control is demonstrated to operate effectively. [PV-04, Closure Validation Principle]
- Phase 5 reporting goes to the accountable committee. Closure of actions is validated through independent evidence testing rather than management assertion. [PV-04, Phase 5 — Reporting and Follow-Up]
- Operating effectiveness testing checks whether controls operated as designed over the review period, using risk-based sampling, analytics over full populations where data permits, and evidence validation. [PV-04, Phase 3 — Operating Effectiveness]
- Findings are rated by risk and traced to root cause rather than symptom. Management actions are agreed with defined owners and dates. [PV-04, Phase 4 — Findings and Root Cause]
- Design effectiveness evaluates whether controls are designed to address the identified risks, using walkthroughs, documentation review and design gap identification. [PV-04, Phase 2 — Design Effectiveness]
- Planning confirms objectives, scope and stakeholders, covers the process, regulatory drivers and control environment, and defines the testing approach, including sampling basis and use of data analytics. [PV-04, Phase 1 — Planning and Risk Assessment]
- Firms pursuing growth in regulated activity often under-invest in control capacity during growth. In practice control capacity is the binding constraint, and supervisors increasingly test whether capability was scaled ahead of volume. [PV-08, The Pattern We See]
- Growth is approved on the basis of a remediation programme reported as complete, where closure evidence has not been independently validated. This is directly relevant to the CL-05 versus EX-07 conflict. [PV-08, Three Failure Modes]
- Control resourcing plans lag the commercial plan by twelve to eighteen months. [PV-08, Three Failure Modes]
- Governance forums are constituted after deployment rather than before, most visibly in the adoption of artificial intelligence. [PV-08, Three Failure Modes]
- Independent validation of remediation closure before the programme is stood down, and an explicit control capacity plan linked to the commercial growth plan, with volume triggers. [PV-08, What Good Looks Like]
- Governance should be established as a precondition of deployment, not a follow-on workstream. Board reporting should distinguish management assertion from independently validated position. [PV-08, What Good Looks Like]

## 2. Conflicts and Reconciliation

**Subject:** Status of the financial crime remediation programme (substantially complete vs. findings still open)

- **Source A:** Board was told the programme is 'substantially complete'. All twelve high-priority findings were remediated and closed with second-line-accepted evidence. Three medium-priority findings remain open. No high-priority findings are open. [CL-05, Item 6 — Financial Crime Remediation Programme]
- **Source B:** Per persons familiar with the supervisory dialogue, supervisors do not regard the programme as complete. Four high-priority findings from the 2024 review remain open, including beneficial ownership verification and sanctions screening tuning. Some findings reported as closed were reopened after independent validation found the closure evidence insufficient. [EX-07, Remediation Status Questioned]
